Before you write

We do not accept guest posts, sponsored articles, or paid link insertions. Our editorial content is independent and reader-supported. We do accept clearly labeled display advertising — contact partners@axisurbanelements.com for details.

Email editorial@axisurbanelements.com with the subject line “Correction” and include the article URL, the specific error, and any supporting sources. We review all correction requests and update articles where warranted.

Short excerpts (up to 150 words) with a clear link back to the original article are welcome. Full reproduction requires written permission. Email hello@axisurbanelements.com with details of your request.

We aim to respond within 2–3 business days. Due to the volume of messages, we cannot always reply to every email — but we do read them all.
