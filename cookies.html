Cookies Policy

This policy explains how Axis Urban Elements uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, and how you can manage your preferences.

In short: We use a minimal set of cookies. Essential cookies keep the site working, and analytics cookies help us understand how readers use our content — but we never track you across other websites or sell your data.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by another domain).

We also use similar technologies such as local storage and pixels, which function in comparable ways. In this policy, "cookies" refers to all these technologies collectively.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential functionality: To remember your cookie preferences and ensure the website functions correctly.
  • Analytics: To understand how visitors interact with our content — which pages are most read, how long readers stay, and which topics resonate. This data is aggregated and anonymized.
  • Performance: To monitor site speed and stability, helping us identify and fix technical issues.

We do not use cookies for advertising, behavioral targeting, or cross-site tracking. We do not sell any data collected through cookies.

3. Types of cookies we use

Cookies on our site fall into two categories:

  • Essential cookies: These are necessary for the website to function. They enable core features like security, network management, and accessibility. You cannot opt out of these while using our site.
  • Analytics cookies: These help us understand how readers use our site. We use a privacy-focused analytics provider that does not track you across other websites and does not collect personally identifiable information. You can opt out of these via your browser settings or our cookie preference tool.

4. Detailed cookie list

The table below lists the specific cookies we use, their purpose, and their duration:

We review our cookie usage regularly to ensure we only collect what is necessary.

5. Managing your preferences

You have several options for controlling cookies:

Browser settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Block all cookies.
  • Block only third-party cookies.
  • Delete cookies when you close your browser.
  • Browse in private/incognito mode, which discards cookies after your session.

Be aware that blocking essential cookies may affect how our website functions. For guidance, refer to your browser's help documentation:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Opt out of analytics

If you prefer not to participate in our analytics, you can disable JavaScript or use a browser extension that blocks tracking scripts. Our analytics data is already anonymized, but we respect your choice.

Your consent

When you first visit our site, you'll see a banner allowing you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in our footer or contacting us directly.

6. Third-party cookies

We keep third-party services to a minimum. The only external service that may set cookies is:

  • Google Fonts: Used to serve typography. Google may collect your IP address as part of this service. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

We do not use third-party advertising networks, social media pixels, or any other cross-site tracking cookies.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology or legislation. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we will provide a more prominent notice, such as a banner on our homepage or an email to subscribers.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
